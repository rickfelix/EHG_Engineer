## 3. Model and effort posture

Encodes two chairman directions of 2026-09-05: bulk reading on cheaper models with a stronger model checking, and **Anthropic models only, on the three Max plans, never API billing** (D2 as revised; this supersedes the Gemini-ladder draft, which was the drafting session's choice, not the chairman's). Gemini keeps the one place EHG already uses it, reading YouTube videos, which is outside Michael's loop. This section becomes `CLAUDE_MICHAEL_MODEL_POSTURE.md`.

**The consequence for venue.** A Max-plan model is only reachable from a Claude Code session. So the durable venue (GHA) does everything that needs no model, and Michael's own seat does everything that does, using Claude Code sub-agents with explicit model overrides so that the cheap tier reads and the strong tier verifies inside one Max-plan session. Michael's seat therefore runs like Adam's and the Coordinator's: a persistent tab under whichever account profile has capacity, restored by the same reboot-respawn and singleton-relaunch machinery, with an overnight tick in addition to the morning conversation. Durability is then a two-layer claim: the GHA layer (fetch, rules-first matching, calendar, Todoist snapshot, assembly, rendering) lands every night regardless; the model layer lands whenever the seat is alive, and when it isn't, the brief degrades honestly (§5) rather than guessing. This is the trade the chairman chose over API billing, and the spec says so.

**Degraded path when the seat is dead overnight.** GHA still produces a verified brief: rules-matched threads are handled, the unmatched remainder is listed under Gmail as "unclassified — N threads Michael didn't get to," Todoist items without an `Est:` line carry no proposed date, and `headsUp` says the seat was down. The singleton-relaunch trigger and reboot-respawn restore the seat; the `michael-seat-uptime` gauge (§9) counts overnight ticks missed. Nothing is silently guessed by a weaker path.

**Account rotation.** The seat launches under any of the three profiles (`relaunchUnderProfile`), and the account-capacity gauge already knows all three plans; Michael's overnight tick is small (tens of sub-agent calls) and his morning conversation is the only sustained use, so rotating him to whichever plan has the most headroom that week is the natural policy. `account_profile` is stamped at registration (§1.3).

**Metering.** Every sub-agent call records `model_used`, `tokens_in`, `tokens_out` into `michael_feeder_runs` via the tick script's summary; conversation spend is self-reported in the ledger until harness `cost_tokens` metering lands. The token-budget gauge is deferred to that SD (§9). The pins are parameters with provenance; if the Sonnet seat misjudges rulings in the parallel-read phase, the posture row changes, with a ratification.

## 11. Acceptance tests

Account independence: Michael launched under each of the three profiles on three consecutive mornings produces a verified brief and ledger entry each day; `michael_brief_runs.data_json` differs only by date-driven content; `account_profile` is stamped on each seat. Durability: **nine days** with no consent re-run and no Claude session still produces a verified (degraded, `finalized = false`) brief every day from the GHA layer alone (this exercises the seven-day refresh-token cliff under D4; under the re-consent posture the test instead asserts `_OAUTH_EXPIRING` fires on day 6 and `oauth-health` trips on day 7). Rule stickiness: a ruling given in conversation on day N is applied by the feeder on day N+1 and appears in `RULES.md` with its ratification id. Verification tier: on a seeded set of 50 labeled threads, every Flash/Pro disagreement is surfaced as `borderline` and none is silently archived. Idempotency: killing `gmail-triage` mid-run and re-running produces no duplicate `threads.modify` calls (mock counts) and completes the intents. Silence: **Michael's session** emits no SMS, email, or coordination row between 07:00 and 03:00 ET on a normal day. Never-claim-more-than-landed: a corrupted `data_json` (NUL byte) yields `verified = false`, a `_BRIEF_MISSING` line, and an opening sentence that says so. Rollback: a thread archived in error is restored by `gmail-act.mjs --unarchive` and the causing rule is superseded with provenance. Retirement: after (I), the grep in §8 step 5 is clean.
