The seat's `brief-finalize.mjs` replaces the lede and Today sentence, applies overnight classifications, re-renders, re-verifies, and stamps `enriched_at`; a brief without it is complete and says in its opening line that overnight enrichment didn't land.

## 7. The morning conversation

Encoded in the `/michael` skill and the contract. Open: read today's `michael_brief_runs`; if absent or unverified after 05:45, say so in one line and offer `brief-assemble.mjs --inline`. Opening, then Gmail then Todoist, one topic per message, at most one judgment call in flight; enrichment offered once near the end; close with the recap and the ledger entry.

Verbs are scripts, invoked by absolute path: `scripts/michael/gmail-act.mjs --thread <id> --label <id> [--archive|--unarchive]` (`--unarchive` also stamps `reopened_at`); `scripts/michael/todoist-act.mjs reschedule|complete|add|comment …` (a reschedule that reverses a proposed date stamps `moved_back_at`); `scripts/michael/rule-encode.mjs --domain … --key … --text "…" [--json '…'] --source terminal:<ref>` (writes `michael_rules` with provenance, supersedes the prior row, regenerates `RULES.md`, and **calls the Opus verifier first whenever the write flips `auto_apply` or supersedes an active rule**); `scripts/michael/closure-add.mjs`; `scripts/michael/capture.mjs`; `scripts/michael/feedback-append.mjs`. The seat never writes tables directly.

**Earned autonomy (as conditioned by Solomon Q4).** No counter is stored. `scripts/michael/autonomy-read.mjs` computes, per `rule_key`, the approval streak from `michael_feedback_ledger.dispositions` at read time (consecutive `chosen: 'approve'` on proposals that rule predicted). At a chairman-set threshold (default 7) the conversation proposes flipping `auto_apply`, allowed only for reversible verbs (label, archive, reschedule; complete and delete never auto-apply), with the Opus verification above. An auto-applied action still writes a disposition row with `chosen: 'auto'`, so the grain survives the flip. **Revocation fires on the outcome signal first:** a `reopened_at` on a thread that rule archived, or a `moved_back_at` on a task it rescheduled, revokes `auto_apply` immediately and stages a rule-edit proposal; three consecutive overrides of proposals is the second trigger. The vision's "reopens from archive stays near zero" is therefore both a success measure and the revoke input.

Silence: outside a chairman-initiated exchange, the seat emits only the quiet-tick's actionable lines. Failures the chairman must know about before he sits down reach him as one line in Adam's 6 AM text (§1.5).

## 9. Gauges

Each gauge names its query, so the gate's completeness check has an instrument per criterion (CAPA rule 49656c8c). Registered in `lib/governance/gauge-registry.js` with `ownerRole: 'michael'` (stub-adoption: `detectorFn: null, enabled: false` first):

`michael-brief-landed` — `select 1 from michael_brief_runs where et_date = today_et() and verified` empty after 06:30 ET. `michael-feeder-health` — any `michael_feeder_runs` row today with `status = 'failed'`, or the same feeder `degraded` on three consecutive dates. `michael-seat-uptime` — count of dates in the last 7 with no `michael_feeder_runs` row where `venue = 'seat'`; trips at 2; **go-live requires ≤ 2 in the parallel-read week**. `michael-oauth-health` — `michael_credentials.last_error = 'invalid_grant'` or `expires_at < now() + interval '48 hours'`. `michael-classifier-drift` — 7-day share of sampled items where `verified_by` disagrees, > 5%. `michael-gmail-modify-ceiling` — any run today with `counts.threads_modified > ceiling`. `michael-surface-rate` — 7-day mean of `needs_you` count above a chairman-set ceiling (default 5). `michael-reopen-rate` — count of `reopened_at` in the last 7 days on threads archived by an `auto_apply` rule; trips at 1 (and revokes, §7). `michael-overdue-cleared` — any `michael_todoist_snapshot` row for a conversation day with `proposed_action` set and `chosen_action` null. `michael-ledger-gap` — a date with `surfaced_at` set and no `michael_feedback_ledger` row. `michael-account-independence` — 14 consecutive seat registrations on one `account_profile`. ## 11. Acceptance tests

Account independence: the seat launched under each of the three profiles on three consecutive mornings produces a verified brief and ledger entry each day; `account_profile` is stamped on each. Durability of the brief of record: nine days with no Claude session still produces a verified brief every day from the host and GHA layers alone. Re-consent posture: run once before go-live — day six raises `michael-oauth-health` (warning) and one line in Adam's text; day seven trips it; the runbook restores within one command. Rule stickiness: a ruling given on day N is applied by the feeder on day N+1 and appears in `RULES.md` with its ratification id. Verification tier: on a seeded set of 50 labeled threads, every Sonnet/Opus disagreement is surfaced as `borderline` and none is silently archived. Modify ceiling: a seeded inbox of 80 newsletter threads yields a `degraded` run with exactly `ceiling` modifications applied. Idempotency: killing `gmail-triage` mid-run and re-running produces no duplicate `threads.modify` calls and completes the intents. Autonomy: seven seeded approvals on one rule make `autonomy-read` propose a flip; one `--unarchive` on a thread that rule archived revokes it and stages a proposal; a `complete` verb can never be flipped. Silence: the seat emits no SMS, email, or coordination row between 07:00 and 03:00 ET on a normal day. Never-claim-more-than-landed: a corrupted `data_json` yields `verified = false`, a `_BRIEF_MISSING` line, and an opening sentence that says so. Handoff: a fleet-class thread produces exactly one `chairman_handoff` row per morning with `origin: 'michael'` that does not raise Adam's directed-row count. Retirement: after (I), `grep -r "_Cowork\|Dropbox" lib scripts .github docs` is clean outside this folder.
