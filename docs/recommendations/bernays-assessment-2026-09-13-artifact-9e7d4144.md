# Bernays and EHG — independent assessment (Solomon, propose-only, 2026-09-13)

Commission: chairman at the Solomon terminal ~12:2xZ. Authorization: analysis and recommendations only. AltifyAI read-only. Budget at entry 90 min / 300k tokens / one read-only survey. Evidence classes used below: [FACT] verified this session from rows, code or git; [ARCH] architectural evidence from the code surveys (file:line); [INFER] inference from facts; [SPEC] speculation; [MEMORY] external scholarship cited from memory, not verified online this session (contract rule: flagged, never silently asserted).

## 1. Executive verdict

ADAPT. Confidence: medium-high on the verdict, medium on the sequencing.

Adopt the research-first, motive-aware, third-party-credibility and honest-campaign principles as discipline inside stages that already exist. Reject the apparatus: engineered consent, front groups, staged pseudo-events, identity exploitation. Defer any new capability build until AltifyAI's demand test has produced its first real event, because today EHG has no demand evidence at all [FACT: campaign_enrollments 0, venture_consent_events 0, publish ledger 0, channel autonomy 0, payments 0, usage events 0 for AltifyAI; outreach to real people BLOCKED until stages 24 and 25 pass, ventures.metadata.outreach_ruling]. The working hypothesis ("better at constructing than at generating demand") is therefore true today by construction, not yet by capability: the demand test has never run. The right first move is to run it honestly, with the positioning hypotheses made explicit, and to let the result decide what capability is worth building.

## 3. Which principles remain valid, which do not

Valid and useful: research first; motives beyond function; disclosed third-party credibility; genuine events and useful public data; messenger and channel fit; narrative consistency; campaign discipline with pre-registered gauges; category framing when true.

Outdated, oversimplified or unacceptable: mass-mind manipulation and the invisible-government premise; front groups and undisclosed sponsorship; staged pseudo-events presented as spontaneous; appeals engineered to exploit fear or identity insecurity; the assumption that a skilled counsel can manufacture demand for anything. Modern evidence [MEMORY] supports the persuasion mechanisms Cialdini catalogued (social proof, authority, consistency, scarcity, unity) as real but bounded, and it treats manufactured versions of them as fraud, not technique.

Venture selection and latent-need discovery: motive evidence at stage 0-5 beyond the LLM critique; today customerNeed 58 and marketFit 60 for AltifyAI are LLM scores [FACT: truth_validation_decision], not observed need. Positioning: three hypotheses instead of one statement; the stage-16 brief has no consumer anywhere in the repo although its docstring claims stages 17 and 18 read it [ARCH]; the agencies-vs-creators inconsistency is what an unread brief produces. Value propositions: functional, emotional, identity as explicit variables. Category creation: a hypothesis field, tested, not asserted. Third-party validation: a provenance-bound proof registry, absent today. Launch and earned attention: a truthful event plan tied to a real data release. Conversion and willingness-to-pay: K1/K2 already exist and are honest; variants can ride them only as informational splits at this sample size. Retention and advocacy: re-engagement email copy exists [FACT: stage 18] but nothing sends it; churn is a forecast input; NPS is "pending_data_collection"; referral is a hypothesis string [ARCH]. ## 6. Absorb or restructure

Absorb: three hypothesis fields in the positioning brief; a proof registry as an artifact type with a provenance rule; a consistency reader at stage 24; the demand test plan carrying hypotheses as variables. All fit existing stages and evidence processes. Structural, and only later: a cross-venture demand-intelligence registry keyed by motive, message, messenger, channel and proof type against real-event outcomes. Existing mechanisms are insufficient because nothing today carries a demand outcome from one venture to the next; but at one venture per month the registry would hold a dozen rows a year, so it is a volume question, not a design question.

## 7. Accumulation into an EHG advantage

Only real_event outcomes accumulate (mock runs excluded by the execution_mode discriminator from Part B). The advantage, if it exists, is the pairing of honest gauges with hypothesis discipline: knowing which motive, message and messenger converted for which kind of customer, with provenance. It is durable only if the registry never admits engagement metrics or unverified proof.

## 8. Separate narratives

Yes, three audiences, three narratives, one evidence base: venture customers (what the product does for them, truthfully); prospective acquirers (evidence of demand, unit economics, transferability, the same facts re-emphasized); EHG itself (a governed, repeatable system). The EHG narrative must follow venture results, never precede them: there is no live venture yet, and a factory narrative without a venture result is the "factory becomes the venture" pattern in prose.

## 9. Legitimate persuasion versus manipulation

Tests, in order: disclosure (every sponsorship and relationship visible); provenance (every proof traceable to a real, consenting, independent source); symmetry (would the customer act the same knowing everything EHG knows); gauge (conversion, willingness to pay, retention, never engagement); vulnerability (no fear or urgency beyond verifiable fact, no targeting by vulnerability); independence (kill thresholds pre-registered before any message change, variants cannot move them); the existing honest-gauge rule (no verdict without a met floor). Vanity engagement mistaken for demand is prevented by the gauge test; persuasive messaging disguising weak fit is prevented by the independence test.

## 10. Could the factory become the venture again, as a marketing apparatus

Yes, and the precedent is measured: the demand engine already has a ladder, invariants, a ledger and an autonomy model with zero real events [FACT]. A "demand-intelligence capability" built before the first demand test would be the same pattern. Mitigation: gate any capability build on a measured K1/K2 row and on at least three ventures with demand data.

## Read-only AltifyAI illustration

Functional hypothesis: hours saved writing alt text and better SEO [FACT: positioning brief, hero]. Emotional hypothesis: relief from tedium and reduced legal exposure [FACT: venture description names "legal exposure"; the demand-test page sells WCAG compliance reports to agencies]. Identity hypothesis: the professional who ships accessible content [INFER: implied by brand voice, never stated]. Evidence available to evaluate them: none yet [FACT: K1/K2 unmeasured, test not started]. Evidence that could evaluate them: the K1 gauge (landing to qualified signup, floor 300 visitors, proceed at 4 percent) and K2 (card-verified preorders on 30 touches). Split three ways, 300 visitors yield about 100 per variant and two to four conversions each, so the variants cannot be statistically separated at the planned scale [INFER]. Conclusion: stronger messaging cannot be distinguished from product-market fit in this test; the honest-gauge rule is what keeps the pooled result from being read as either. The one actionable observation is the mismatch between the agency-targeted test page and the creator-targeted product; that is a positioning question, and no gate reads it. No change to lifecycle, sequence, promotion, evidence requirements or kill criteria is proposed.

## What EHG should explicitly prohibit

Fake or LLM-written reviews, testimonials and quotes; undisclosed sponsorship or affiliation; astroturfing and sock puppets; synthetic personas presented publicly as customers; fear or urgency appeals not grounded in verifiable fact; targeting by vulnerability; message changes after kill thresholds are set that alter the thresholds; engagement metrics as demand evidence; outreach to real people before go-live (existing ruling); dark patterns in conversion flows.

## Smallest reversible experiment, for later consideration only

Inside the AltifyAI demand test as already ratified: pre-register three headline variants (functional, emotional, identity) with an equal traffic split; keep K1 and K2 on the pooled total so variants cannot move the kill; record per-variant conversion as informational. Reversible by removing the variants; the pooled test is unchanged. Requires chairman authorization; not authorized by this commission.

## Recommendation by timing

Useful now, as discipline with no build: name the three hypotheses in the review of AltifyAI's demand test plan; reconcile the agency-versus-creator positioning before the test starts; apply the prohibitions as review criteria. Useful after AltifyAI: proof registry with provenance; hypothesis variables in the demand test plan; messenger map; consistency reader. Useful only at greater venture volume: the cross-venture demand-intelligence registry; category creation playbook; acquirer narrative program. Never appropriate: front groups, manufactured proof, staged pseudo-events, identity exploitation, undisclosed persuasion, engagement as demand.

## Strongest objections

To the working hypothesis: EHG has no demand evidence, so its relative strength at construction is untested; the gap to close is one honest demand test, not a capability. At one venture per month, learning cannot accumulate for years. Bernays's influence is overstated by his own memoirs [MEMORY]; modern evidence weighs product, distribution and price above narrative. To my recommendation: Adapt can still grow into an apparatus, and the mitigation depends on the chairman holding the gate. The prohibitions cannot all be enforced in code and rest on review. My external citations are from memory and unverified online this session. A truthful narrative may lose to competitors who manipulate; EHG accepts that by design.
